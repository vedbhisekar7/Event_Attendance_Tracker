A little guide to Gather

01

Choose your event

Switch events with the selector or create a new one. The demo event contains 48 fictional registrations so you can explore safely.

02

Build the guest list

Add participants one at a time or import a CSV. Name, College ID, and email are required. College IDs and emails must be unique within an event.

03

Find. Verify. Welcome.

At the check-in desk, search by name, email, College ID, or phone. Open a student’s record, compare their physical card, then mark them present. A mistaken check-in can be undone.

04

Keep an eye on the room

The overview shows totals and attendance by year or branch. Filter the directory and export a CSV report. Counts refresh every 30 seconds and after your changes.

Attendance lives in data/attendance.sqlite3 on the server. Refreshing or closing the browser does not erase it. This coursework app has no login—keep real student data on a trusted local machine.
